# Boss cinematics and HP checkpoints

Production integration of approved local preview: 14-second door/walk/weighted horizontal cut/overhead stab introduction. The existing battle phase, encounter identity and railway unlock remain compatible. Client uses serverTime + monotonic elapsed time, focuses all arena players on the boss, hides the regular boss during the scene, cancels click paths and input queues, and restores each player's follow camera afterward. Introductory scar is cosmetic. Existing floor texture is fractured at its original 48px cell size.

HP checkpoints: 2/3 and 1/3 of maximum, once each attempt. Attack damage is capped at the next checkpoint so a high-damage hit cannot bypass a scene. The shared room snapshot records smashCount and smash {at,duration=3800,x,y,tiles,knockbacks,applied}. At 1800ms, the server applies one cardinal-cell knockback per arena player and adds exactly three new blocked floor tiles. Safe destinations are reserved before choosing the broken cells; walls, other players, enemies and existing rubble are excluded. Completely boxed-in characters are never pushed into a wall. All arena gameplay mutations are rejected server-side while opening or smashing; read/poll/chat remain possible. Enemy turns in the arena are paused. Polls never apply the same knockback twice. Room state persists across reloads. Full wipe resets broken floors and checkpoint state for the next attempt; rewards and accounts are unchanged.

Migration: supabase/migrations/202611240001_online_boss_cinematics.sql. Private predecessor functions stay non-executable by authenticated clients. Verified live opening-duration hook and guard existence, and bypass_allowed=false. No active boss room was present at application time.

Validation: PGlite two-character test covers shared opening, both players' mutation lock, full intro duration, actual attacks stopping at 320/160 HP, two three-cell fractures, one-cell non-overlapping knockbacks, blocked rubble, repeated-poll idempotence, and full-wipe reset. Station round-trip regression passed. Muted browser QA checked actual room rendering, intro input lock, phase cameras, cracked floor and camera return; no console errors. Physical iPhone / separate real devices were not used for QA. Production build passed.

Art: approved built-in image_gen walk and action atlases from public/assets/online/boss-intro-demo-v2; original prompts and prototype history are documented in docs/boss-intro-demo-20261124.md. No new generated art in this integration.
