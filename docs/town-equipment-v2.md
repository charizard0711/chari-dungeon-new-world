- Remove the gacha card, exploration and shop buttons from the town sidebar. The gacha machine is at (7,9), merchant Mira at (18,9), and guide Leaf at (16,11). Approach within Manhattan distance two and right-click. NPCs show a speech cursor only in range. Both new service cells are blocked on the client and server.
- Replace online inventory/equipment picking with a fixed icon grid, selected-item detail/comparison panel, custom scrollbars, and equip/use/drop actions. The window fits the game canvas, including the mobile layout. Modal input pauses the underlying Phaser input to prevent click-through.
- Add one necklace per grade D/C/B/A/S/SS/SSS, giving defense +1/+2/+3/+5/+7/+9/+12. Add its equipment slot below the weapon; mobile equipment uses four compact slots.
- Add light/dark versions of every existing elemental weapon type/grade, including event tiers: 34 weapons and 10 shields. Copy the matching tier's base stats; use ordinary type passives rather than copying quest/event-only powers. Light/dark are reciprocal weaknesses (×1.5), same-element resistance remains ×0.75.
- Keep per-copy equipment identity and durability. A zero-durability weapon/shield is unequipped and cannot be equipped until repaired, but remains owned. Dropping/picking it up preserves its UUID and durability. Merchant repair costs ceil(missing durability / 10) G, minimum 1 G; repair stones restore 50 durability. New light/dark held weapons, arrows and katana sheaths use matching assets.
- Give each character 300 G once on schema upgrade (and as the new-character default). Gacha costs 100 G; D/C/B/A/S/SS/SSS odds are 35/25/18/12/6/3/1 percent. Eligible weapons have a 1% color-variant roll. Prices, balance checks, random rewards and service distance checks are server-controlled.
- Provide twenty persistent quick slots (1–0 and Shift+1–0). Drag owned consumables/equipment from inventory onto them; right-click a slot to clear. Consumables disappear from all assigned slots at zero count; dropped/sold equipment loses its own shortcut. Pointer-based drag supports mouse and touch without triggering the map underneath.
- Add twinkling effects for color-variant held weapons, inventory icons, quick slots and the HUD weapon icon.
