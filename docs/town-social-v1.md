# Town friends, gold and equipment polish

- Account-level friend requests by exact character name; incoming approval/rejection, outgoing cancellation and removal. Names and online/offline presence only, no account/email identifiers. Relationships are private and capped at 100 per account. All actions authenticate the caller and enforce participant/recipient ownership; direct table writes and the old world wrapper are revoked.
- Gold appears as a coin icon in inventory. Drop a positive integer up to the balance; a ground pile retains the amount and transfers it once to the picker. Range, ownership, balance, integer bounds and overflow are checked by the server. Starter balances are unchanged.
- Quick slots swap atomically with one server action when dragged onto each other; empty slots accept moves. Dragging never uses the source item. Inventory assignment remains copy-to-shortcut. Other modals hide the quickbar and necklace slot; only inventory leaves the quickbar accessible. DOM modals use the golden game cursor.
- Normalize added weapon item art to the established lower-left to upper-right format, remove neighboring-cell fragments during alpha cropping, and use separate directional held atlases. Added starred weapons use their own held atlas/grip instead of rotating the inventory icon. New shields also use centered held layers. Existing weapon acquisition from shop/gacha has a one-percent variant chance; pickup transfers preserve existing variants. Super-account catalog lets the player select normal or color variant before claiming; starred icons and held weapons sparkle.
- Short metallic equip and necklace chime WAV assets are generated by `scripts/build-equipment-sounds.mjs`. Sound is played only after an actual successful gear change and honors current SE/master/noAudio/mute settings. Tests never play audio.
